About

I work on string phenomenology: building and classifying four-dimensional string models, and asking which of them can reproduce the physics we actually observe.

My research covers heterotic string model building in the free-fermionic and orbifold formulations, non-supersymmetric string vacua and the decompactification problem, grand unified theories, neutrino masses and proton stability, and string effective actions with higher-curvature gravity. Because the space of candidate models is enormous, much of this work is computational — including genetic algorithms and, more recently, quantum annealing.

I have been at the University of Ioannina since 1996, following postdoctoral positions at the École Polytechnique and SISSA/ISAS, and visiting appointments in Paris, Trieste and CERN. I served as Head of the Department of Physics from 2013 to 2017.
